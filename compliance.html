Licenses & Certifications

Some products we sell require state or EPA-mandated training. Keep your licenses current here so you can order without delays.

Private Applicator License

Issued by your state. Required for restricted-use pesticides on your own farm.

Not on File
Need a Private Applicator License? Contact your state Department of Agriculture or local extension office. In Colorado: CO Dept of Ag.

Commercial Applicator License

Required for commercial pesticide applicators applying for hire.

Not on File

Paraquat Training Certification

EPA-required for purchasing or applying any product containing Paraquat. Valid for 3 years from completion.

Not Completed
How to complete the training (free): Go through the EPA's online Paraquat training course. It takes about an hour. Save your certificate, then enter the certificate number and date below.

Take EPA Paraquat Training

Dicamba Training

Required annually for products containing Dicamba (XtendiMax, Engenia, Tavium, etc.).

Not Completed
How to complete the training: Annual training is offered by manufacturers (BASF, Bayer, Syngenta) and state extension services. Most are online and free.
