First time paying us through Stripe? Here's what to expect.

After you click a payment option above, you'll be sent to Stripe's secure checkout.

Pay by Card — fastest. Payment goes through in seconds. Includes a 3.5% convenience fee.

Pay by ACH (bank transfer) — free, and preferred for larger invoices. Most banks support "instant verification" — you'll log in through Stripe's secure window and the payment is on its way in a day or two.

If your bank doesn't support instant verification, Stripe falls back to micro-deposit verification: they'll deposit $0.01 into your account (shows up the next business day), you'll find a 6-digit code starting with "SM" in that transaction's description on your bank statement, come back to the Stripe email you got, and enter it to confirm the account. One-time step — future payments to AcreProfit won't need it again.

⚠️ About phishing: Stripe will only email you about verifying a bank account after you've started a payment yourself. If you get a "Verify your bank account for AcreProfit" email out of the blue — one you didn't initiate — don't click any links. Forward it to contact@acreprofit.com and we'll check our end.
